About This Website

Welcome to the official website of Nikesh Paudel — a Nepali motivational speaker, certified life coach, author, poet and storyteller based in Kathmandu, Nepal. This platform is a genuine space for original Nepali literature, personal development writing, and life coaching insights crafted from years of real lived experience and heartfelt human connection.

The blog publishes original articles in both Nepali and English, covering deeply human topics. You can explore Nepali poems (kavita) that speak to the heart, Nepali gazals written in the lyrical sher-o-shairi tradition, short stories (kahani) that carry timeless wisdom, novels (upanyas) exploring the depth of Nepali life, motivational articles grounded in real human struggle, and inspirational quotes rooted in Nepali culture and philosophy.

Every piece of content on this website is written personally by Nikesh Paudel — authentic, thoughtful, and deeply connected to the Nepali experience. Whether you are a reader of Nepali literature, a student seeking motivation, a professional looking for personal growth guidance, or someone navigating a difficult chapter in life, this blog offers something meaningful for you.

Nikesh Paudel has been speaking at personal development events, schools, and colleges across Nepal, inspiring thousands of young Nepalis to embrace their potential. As a motivational speaker, he brings warmth, honesty and practical wisdom to every talk. As a life coach, he offers one-on-one guidance sessions both in-person in Kathmandu and online worldwide. His writing bridges the poetic traditions of Nepal with the warmth of modern life coaching, creating a body of work that is both culturally grounded and universally relatable.

Explore the full blog archive, read the about page to learn more about Nikesh's journey, or reach out through the contact page for bookings, collaborations, or coaching inquiries.

About the Author

Nikesh Paudel निकेश पौडेल

Nepali Author · Poet · Gazal Writer · Motivational Speaker · Life Coach

Location: Kathmandu, Bagmati Province, Nepal  |  Contact: support@nikeshpaudel.com.np

Nikesh Paudel is a Nepali author, poet, and motivational speaker based in Kathmandu. He writes with an intimate understanding of Nepali culture, society, and the everyday emotional landscape of Nepali people — in both the Nepali and English languages.

As a poet and gazal writer, Nikesh draws from the rich tradition of South Asian lyric poetry while giving it a distinctly Nepali identity. His poems and gazals explore love, loss, resilience, identity, and the search for meaning — themes that resonate deeply across generations. As a motivational writer and life coach, he helps individuals navigate life's challenges with clarity and genuine human warmth.

Nikesh offers one-on-one life coaching sessions both in-person in Kathmandu and online worldwide, and is available for motivational speaking engagements, literary events, and collaborative projects.
